# How I think about a draft

Free text. Written by the commissioner, read by Clams AI as his stated
views. Anything below the line is treated as his opinion and is allowed
to drive a grade — unlike the roster facts, which only describe what
happened.

Everything above the line is guidance for whoever is editing the file and
is never sent to the model. Write below it, the way you'd say it out
loud. It reads better than notes.

---

## Quarterbacks

Roster construction starts with getting one of the pocket quarterbacks in
this league, due to the scoring settings, preferably for cheap. I love a
Joe Burrow, Dak Prescott for this reason.

I don't like paying a premium for the ones that are more known for their
rushing in other leagues, like Lamar or Jalen Hurts, just due to scoring
settings. Cheap is a different question — it's the price I object to, not
the player.

I also don't like paying a lot for a backup.

**Why the position matters here at all.** A valuable quarterback is a
valuable quarterback. Last season Josh Allen scored 511 points, while
CMC — the top flex player — scored 369. Matt Stafford scored 564. That is
the gap this league's scoring creates, and it's the reason a quarterback
is worth having rather than an afterthought.

So Josh Allen shouldn't go in the first round or so, but he's worth more
than his price after that.

## Running backs

You have to get 1 or 2 early, since this league tends to draft them high.

However, there are some teams where you can get a guy that's got a chance
to be the lead back in a backfield that's a little unclear, for not that
much. That's appealing on the bench.

## Wide receivers

You better have a couple of your starters in the first six rounds.
Otherwise you just don't have that many WRs come up the depth chart like
you do on RBs — it's more skill based than opportunity based.

## Tight ends

I'm good with either paying for the premium guys or otherwise waiting.
No need to use a 6th/7th rounder for some of the guys when they're not
measurably different than the 10th-11th round guys.

## Defense

Isn't really worth paying for at all. So hard to have any continuity from
year to year.
