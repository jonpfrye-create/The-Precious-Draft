# Guys I like, guys I don't

Free text. Read by Clams AI as the commissioner's own opinions on
specific players — the closest thing it has to his rankings.

Everything above the line is guidance for whoever is editing the file and
is never sent to the model. Write below it.

The reason matters more than the name. "I don't trust him" teaches more
than a list, because it transfers to players who aren't on the list.

ADPs are half-PPR, 12 team, as of 14 Aug 2026. Injury and camp notes are
dated where they're the whole point — they go stale fastest, and a
confident claim about a player's health in December that was true in
August is worse than saying nothing.

## I'd take these earlier than ADP

**Jahmyr Gibbs** (RB, DET) — going 1.02
**Bijan Robinson** (RB, ATL) — going 1.02
Both studs, so great 1.1 or 1.2 picks. Even if picking them first doesn't
take any skill, still very nice to have as a foundation.

**Justin Jefferson** (WR, MIN) — going 2.03
Has a quarterback now and is potentially the best WR in the game still.

**George Pickens** (WR, DAL) — going 2.07
As good and productive as CeeDee, and that offense is going to pass a ton.

**Kenneth Walker** (RB, KC) — going 3.02
K9 is the truth, I love him so much. Chiefs interior OL is much better
than the Seahawks.

**Josh Allen** (QB, BUF) — going 3.03
A stud in any league, including ours. He shouldn't go in the first round
or so, but he scored 511 points last year while CMC, the top flex player,
scored 369. A valuable quarterback is a valuable quarterback.

**Ladd McConkey** (WR, LAC) — going 4.08
Should have way more opportunities — and Herbert as well — now that they
have their tackles back.

**Joe Burrow** (QB, CIN) — going 5.09
Healthy, and the king in our league.

**Dak Prescott** (QB, DAL) — going 6.07
Right behind Burrow.

**De'Zhaun Stribling** (WR, SF) — going 13.03
Has been a pre-season stud and they don't have any other healthy pass
catchers.

**Denzel Boston** (WR, CLE) — going 13.03
Lot of buzz around him in camp, they'll be playing from behind, and I
loved him at UW.

**Jordan Love** (QB, GB) — going 13.05
Great in our league and a steal at that price.

**Jonah Coleman** (RB, DEN) — going 13.06
Already impressing Payton, great OL, and JK Dobbins already hurt.

**Chris Rodriguez** (RB, JAX) — going 14.02
Has as much of a chance to be the guy as Tuten, in a good offense, at a
cheap price.

**Ja'Kobi Lane** (WR, BAL) — going 14.07
Getting tons of camp buzz.

**Mike Washington** (RB, LV) — going 14.10
Had some insane measurables, Raiders improved their OL, and it seems like
he'd be the guy if Jeanty went down.

### Liked with a real caveat

**Matt Stafford** (QB, LAR)
Scored 564 points last year, which was even more valuable than Allen, and
I probably should have had him on the positive list. But I don't think
there's much chance he replicates it, with a stronger defense and
off-field questions around his left tackle and his top receiver.

## I wouldn't touch these at that price

**DJ Moore** (WR, BUF) — going 5.01

**Alec Pierce** (WR, IND) — going 5.05
Injury concerns.

**Carnell Tate** (WR, TEN) — going 7.03

## Note on the mid-round receivers

Several of the players above are wide receivers going in rounds 4 to 7.
That is not a view about the tier — it's these individuals. Getting a
couple of starting receivers inside the first six rounds is still the
plan.
